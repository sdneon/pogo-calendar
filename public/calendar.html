How to use PoGo Calendar

Making it your own

  • Add target Pokémon: click a day and add the Pokémon you want to go for there. You can pick a form, e.g. Giratina (Origin). ☆ stars a target, which gets a rainbow ring, and you can give it a comment that shows when you hover it.
  • Edit any event (title, times, Pokémon, how it is shown), or + Add event for your own. Undo my changes puts an event back as it was.
  • Plan rest days: tick Rest day in a day, and add a note if you like. Grey days have nothing major on, so they are good candidates.
  • Everything you change is saved only in this browser on this device. Nobody else sees it, and it is not sent anywhere. Clearing your browser data removes it, so keep a copy with Export.

Reading the calendar

  • Big icons are the Pokémon of events that draw crowds: Raid Hour, Spotlight Hour, Raid Days, Max Battle Days, Max Monday, Community Day and global GO Fest / Tour / Wild Area days.
  • Small icons in a day's bottom-right corner are the week's raid bosses; the thin bars show how long each rotation runs. A Max Monday's bar runs all week, as its Dynamax Pokémon stays in Max Battles.
  • The pin at an icon's bottom-right is the activity:
    Also on icons:
  • Hover an icon for a bigger picture, its type(s), whether a Mega can reach Super Max (Mega Level 4), the hundo catch CP (normal and weather boosted), event times, and for region-locked Pokémon when the event is live in their region.
  • Day colours: super major event · overseas event (✈, remote passes needed for raids; times converted to SGT) · rest day · nothing major.
  • Click a day for everything on it, including smaller events; click any date again to close it. Tap a pin in the legend to hide that activity.

Sharing

  • Export saves your changes (rest days, targets, stars, comments, event edits) as a small file.
  • Import adds a file like that, from another device or a friend, to your calendar. Nothing already there is removed: rest days are combined, notes are appended, and targets are added.

Event data and icons come from Leek Duck and are refreshed every few hours. Times are Singapore time (SGT).
